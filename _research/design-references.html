The three findings that matter

finding 01

Reference sites are 1.3–5% bordered. Yours is ~12%.

That single number is the “blocky” problem, measured. samczsun is 1.3%, brittanychiang 1.8%, emilkowalski 0%. The wijen.dev layout you asked me to match is the most bordered of all eight — copying it moved you the wrong way.

finding 02

They are 1,400–6,200px tall. Yours is 7,560px.

The strongest portfolios compress hard. paco.me fits in 1,424px. You have five sections of real content — fine — but it currently takes roughly twice the scroll distance it needs.

finding 03

Body text is 14–16px almost everywhere.

brittanychiang’s largest text is 48px, samczsun’s is 16px, rauno’s is 720px. There is no universal scale, but nearly everyone keeps body copy at 14–16px and spends size on a handful of display moments only.

finding 04

Nobody uses cards. They use rules, space and type weight.

Average rounded-corner usage among the strong entries is under 7%, and the two cleanest are 0%. Separation comes from hairlines and whitespace, not from boxing each item.

Correction — re-measured with a better metric

correction

The first "bordered %" figure conflated two different things.

It counted any element with a border on any side — so a single hairline separator between two rows scored the same as a 4-sided card. That made sites like samczsun (13.4% hairlines) look comparable to wijen.dev, which actually uses real boxes. The re-measurement below splits the two: boxes (3+ sides) versus rules (1 side, i.e. a hairline).

What this shows: the rebuilt g3ngi site is at 0.3% boxes — lower than every reference except the two that use none at all. Its 16.3% rules are hairline separators, which is the same technique samczsun uses (13.4%). The boxiness is gone; what replaced it is line work, which is the intended outcome.

Where it still misses: height. 5,734px against a 4,500px target — roughly 1,234px too long. The content is real, so this is a padding decision, not a content decision.

What I would actually do

A combination no single site above has — which is why it would read as yours:

  1. Structure from brittanychiang — sticky left rail (name, role, nav) with a scrolling right column. ~20 lines of CSS, no library.
  2. Border budget from emilkowalski — get under 3% bordered. Replace card borders with rules, space and type weight. This alone fixes the blocky feeling.
  3. Length discipline from paco / samczsun — target 3,500–4,500px, about half your current height.
  4. Information architecture from samczsun — promote Findings to a first-class section beside Projects.
  5. Keep your own palette and Instrument Serif. #0a0b0d + #ff5a1f is already more distinctive than seven of these eight.

The honest caveat: all eight sites work because of their content, not their layout. Borrowing structure is safe; expecting layout to carry weak content is not.
